Install

  • Download the signed and notarized DMG from Releases, drag EchoDiar to Applications.
  • Needs macOS 15+ on Apple Silicon. Apple Speech and Apple Foundation Models need macOS 26.
  • EchoDiar lives in the menu bar. Its Dock icon shows while one of its windows is open.
  • Updates itself: menu bar icon > Check for Updates…

Permissions

  • Microphone: records your voice.
  • System Audio Recording: records the meeting's audio. The first-run onboarding tests it and opens the right Privacy & Security pane.
  • Accessibility (optional): only to keep the floating indicator on the meeting window.
  • Calendars (optional): reads your meetings from macOS Calendar to offer recording when they start and to name the recording, and adds the events you create to an "EchoDiar" calendar (On My Mac when available, otherwise your default account, e.g. iCloud, which syncs them). EchoDiar writes to no other calendar.
  • Tell the people you record. Onboarding and Settings > General have a Copy consent message button.

Recording

  • Record one app's audio or all system audio, plus your mic. Mic and system audio go to separate channels of one file.
  • When an app's audio cannot be isolated, EchoDiar records all system audio and says so.
  • Start or stop with Cmd+R in the app, or Cmd+Option+R from any app. Pause with Cmd+Shift+P.
  • Live level meters while recording, and live captions with Transcribe while recording. The menu bar icon shows idle, recording, paused and processing.
  • Pick the microphone, and what happens when it disconnects, in Settings > Recording.
  • Each recording is compressed to AAC (64 kbps by default) once it is transcribed. A recording cut short by a crash is recovered and transcribed at next launch.

Meeting detection

  • Offers to record when a meeting app starts using the mic: Zoom, Teams, Slack, Webex, FaceTime, Discord, Skype, and browsers for web calls such as Meet.
  • Per app: Ask, Always record or Ignore (browsers: Ask or Ignore).
  • A floating indicator shows while recording. Drag it, hide it while EchoDiar is in front, or attach it to the meeting window.
  • Auto-stop: when the meeting app quits, the call goes quiet or the screen locks, the indicator asks before stopping, with a 15 s countdown.

Calendar

  • Works with any account added to macOS Calendar: iCloud, Google, Outlook/Exchange, CalDAV. Set it up in Settings > Recording > Calendar, with the optional Calendars permission.
  • When a meeting starts: Off, Ask (the default: the floating indicator offers Record, Join or Not now), Wait for the call (records the meeting app as soon as the call starts) or Record at start (records all system audio and the mic at the start time).
  • The automatic modes start without asking only for events you organize, accepted or answered Maybe to, and events without invitees on any calendar you can edit, shared ones included (and those you create in EchoDiar). Other invites ask.
  • Choose the calendars to watch, and keep only events with a meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex).
  • Stop when the event ends, plus a few minutes (10 by default). The indicator asks before stopping, with a 15 s countdown.
  • A recording started from an event is named after it.
  • The Calendar page (toolbar button next to Settings) lists the meetings of the next 7 days (updated automatically) with Join, Record and Skip. The menu bar menu lists upcoming meetings.
  • New Event… on the Calendar page creates a meeting: title, date, start and end, and an optional meeting link (https). It goes to an "EchoDiar" calendar (On My Mac when available, otherwise your default account, e.g. iCloud, which syncs those events like any other).
  • Repeat: None, Daily, Weekdays, Weekly or Monthly, with End Repeat never or on a date.
  • Edit… on events in the EchoDiar calendar changes them, and Delete Event… is in the editor. For a repeating event, choose This Event Only or This and Future Events; when Repeat or End Repeat changed, Save offers only This and Future Events.
  • Events in the EchoDiar calendar count even without a meeting link, and follow the same When a meeting starts mode.
  • Works while EchoDiar runs, so turn on Launch at login in Settings > General.

Transcription

  • Starts on its own: while you record with Transcribe while recording, otherwise after Stop. Turn it off with Transcribe automatically in Settings > Recording.
  • Engines: WhisperKit, Parakeet, Qwen3-ASR, and Apple Speech on macOS 26. WhisperKit, Parakeet and Qwen3-ASR models are downloaded only when you click Download in Settings > Transcription; macOS installs Apple Speech language assets the first time you use a language.
  • Speakers: your mic is Me. Turn on Tell speakers apart in Settings > Recording (or Settings > Transcription > Diarization model) to label each voice in the meeting audio: Speaker 1, Speaker 2… Set the number of other speakers there as a hint. Off (the default), the meeting audio is one speaker, Speaker 1, and cloud transcription is asked for no speaker labels.
  • Transcribe while recording (on by default, WhisperKit and Parakeet): transcribes during the recording and shows live captions, so the transcript is ready seconds after Stop. Uses more CPU and memory while recording. Turn it off in Settings > Transcription > General. See How it works.
  • Re-transcribe a conversation with another engine or language.
  • Can wait while the Mac is hot or in Low Power Mode: what is left after Stop is done once the Mac cools down or leaves Low Power Mode.

How it works

  • With Transcribe while recording on (the default), the recording is transcribed while it happens. Each channel, your mic and the meeting audio, is cut into chunks at pauses in speech, at most the model's window (30 s for Whisper); with no pause, the cut falls on the quietest moment.
  • Chunks wait in a queue that keeps up to about 10 minutes of audio, and one speech model, loaded once for the recording, transcribes them. Its lines are the live captions, Me and Others, and they become the saved transcript. WhisperKit and Parakeet only.
  • Nothing is lost: chunks held while the Mac is hot or in Low Power Mode (with Wait when the Mac is hot or in Low Power Mode on) stay in the queue and are transcribed once the Mac recovers. Chunks beyond the queue, chunks that fail, and chunks still held at Stop become gaps, transcribed from the recording after Stop.

On, the speaker model runs after Stop and labels Speaker 1, Speaker 2…: about 10 s for the same meeting.
  • The transcript is made from the uncompressed audio, which is slightly more accurate: 0.0 % word error rate on the same meeting, against about 1 % from the compressed file.

Cloud transcription

  • Optional: OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, AssemblyAI or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Set up ElevenLabs, Deepgram and AssemblyAI in Settings > Transcription > Transcription model, the others in Settings > AI.
  • The first run for each provider and address asks before any audio leaves your Mac. A changed URL asks again.
  • The mic and system channels are uploaded, and billed, separately.
  • API keys are stored in the Keychain.

Transcript & playback

  • Click a line to hear it. Space plays or pauses, Esc goes back.
  • Edit the title, rename speakers, copy the transcript or reveal the folder in Finder.

AI summaries & chat

  • Open the AI panel with Cmd+I. Generate a summary or ask questions about a conversation.
  • Answers cite [mm:ss] timestamps. Click one to jump to that moment in the transcript and recording.
  • Response language, for summaries and answers: automatic (the transcript's language for summaries, the question's for answers), English, French or Vietnamese.
  • On-device: MLX models, and Apple Foundation Models on macOS 26.
  • Local servers: LM Studio, Ollama, Jan, llama.cpp, vLLM. Scan finds the ones running on this Mac.
  • Cloud APIs: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Gemini, Mistral, Azure OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek or a custom endpoint. Keys go in the Keychain.
  • Subscription CLIs: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Gemini CLI, with your own login.
  • Copy as prompt copies the transcript and your question, ready to paste into any chat app.

Library & search

  • Conversations grouped by day, with their size on disk.
  • Rename, reveal in Finder or move to the Trash.
  • Search titles and transcripts with Cmd+F.

Storage

  • Data lives in ~/Library/Application Support/EchoDiar. Settings > Storage shows the space used.
  • Auto-delete: once a day, moves transcribed conversations older than an age you set to the Trash, whole or audio only.
  • The uncompressed audio (audio.caf) is kept until the transcript is written, then compressed to audio.m4a and deleted. If transcription can't start right away, the audio is compressed first.
  • Optionally delete the audio once a transcription succeeds: with Delete audio after transcription, a new recording's uncompressed audio is moved to the Trash instead of being compressed. The transcript, summary and chat stay.
  • Choose the compression bitrate, or keep the uncompressed audio too.

Settings & shortcuts

  • Open with Cmd+,. Sections: General, Recording, Transcription, AI, Storage, Shortcuts, About.
  • Change the global record shortcut, and view the app's shortcuts, in Settings > Shortcuts.
  • Launch at login and the floating indicator are in Settings > General.
  • Default language, Remember last record language (off by default: keeps the language picked in the Record button's menu for the next recordings), Transcribe while recording and Free unused models after are in Settings > Transcription > General. Tell speakers apart is in both Settings > Recording and Settings > Transcription > Diarization model.
