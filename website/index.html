EchoDiar

Record any meeting on your Mac, see who said what, and ask AI about it — local-first and lightweight.

Download Download for Windows GitHub

macOS 15+ · Apple Silicon · Windows 11 (x64, Arm64) · Free & open source (AGPLv3)

Features

  • Lightweight and native: a Swift app that lives in the menu bar. Models load only while they work.
  • Private: transcription and AI can run fully on your Mac. Audio goes to the cloud only if you choose a cloud engine.
  • Any meeting app, any AI: record Zoom, Teams, Meet in a browser or all system audio. Ask local models, cloud APIs or your Claude Code or Codex subscription.
  • Who said what: your mic and the meeting audio on separate channels, the other speakers told apart and renamable.
  • On-device transcription: WhisperKit, Parakeet, Qwen3-ASR or Apple Speech (macOS 26). WhisperKit and Parakeet transcribe while you record, with live captions, so the transcript is ready seconds after you stop.
  • AI summaries and chat: answers cite timestamps that jump to that moment in the recording.
  • Copy as prompt: the transcript and your question, ready to paste into any chat app.
  • Meeting detection: offers to record when a call starts, shows a floating indicator and offers to stop when the meeting ends.
  • Library and search: conversations by day, search over titles and transcripts, auto-delete of old recordings.

Light on your Mac

EchoDiar stays out of the way of your meeting. Measured with the release build on an M4 Pro Mac, recording a call:

While EchoDiar isMemoryCPU
Waiting in the menu bar16 MB0%
Recording, with the floating indicator30 MB3.5%
Recording, with the main window open53 MB8.5%
  • Models load only while they work: transcription, speaker and AI models are freed as soon as their job ends, and their memory goes back to macOS.
  • Transcribing while you record (on by default) keeps the speech model loaded: on top of the costs above, it takes about 190 MB more memory and about 2% more of one core (debug build, averaged over a 15-minute meeting), freed while a recording stays paused.

CPU is a share of one core, averaged over 30 seconds. Numbers vary with the Mac and the meeting.

Transcription

  • On your Mac: WhisperKit, Parakeet, Qwen3-ASR, and Apple Speech on macOS 26.
  • Speakers: FluidAudio diarization of the meeting audio, when "Tell speakers apart" is on (off by default).
  • Transcribe while recording: WhisperKit or Parakeet transcribes each channel as you speak, and the live captions come from it. For a 15-minute call, the transcript is ready 0.8 seconds after Stop (about 10 seconds with speakers told apart) instead of about a minute and a half. Other engines transcribe the whole recording after Stop.
  • Cloud (optional): OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, AssemblyAI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Asks once before audio goes to each one.

AI providers

  • On-device: MLX models, and Apple Foundation Models on macOS 26.
  • Local servers: LM Studio, Ollama, Jan, llama.cpp and vLLM.
  • Cloud APIs: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Gemini, Mistral, Azure OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek or a custom endpoint, with your own key.
  • Subscription CLIs: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Gemini CLI.

Get started

  1. Download the signed and notarized DMG from the releases page and drag EchoDiar to Applications.
  2. Allow the microphone and System Audio Recording, then download a model in Settings > Transcription.
  3. Start and stop recording from any app with Cmd+Option+R. The transcript follows.

More in the docs. EchoDiar updates itself.

On Windows 11: download EchoDiar.appinstaller and open the downloaded file. App Installer installs EchoDiar and keeps it up to date (install steps).
